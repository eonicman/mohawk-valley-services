Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aria work?

Aria connects to your business phone number. When a call comes in, Aria answers professionally, understands what the caller needs using AI, and handles it — taking messages, booking appointments, answering questions about your hours and services, or routing urgent calls to you.

How long does setup take?

About 10 minutes. You answer questions about your business (hours, services, policies), pick a voice, and we connect it to your phone number. Your AI receptionist is live the same day.

Can callers tell it's AI?

Aria uses natural-sounding voice technology. Most callers don't realize they're talking to AI — she sounds like a professional receptionist who knows your business inside and out.

What happens if Aria can't help?

Aria transfers the call to you or a team member, takes a detailed message, and sends you both a text and email notification. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Do I need any special equipment?

No. Aria works with your existing phone number. You don't need new hardware, software, or a landline. It works with cell phones, VoIP, and landlines.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There's no long-term contract. You can cancel or change plans at any time. The setup fee covers your initial configuration and training.
