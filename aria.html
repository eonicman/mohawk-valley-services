Frequently Asked Questions About AI Receptionists

How does an AI phone receptionist work?

Aria connects to your business phone number. When a call comes in, Aria answers professionally, understands what the caller needs using AI, and handles it — taking messages, booking appointments, answering questions about your hours and services, or routing urgent calls to you. Setup takes about 10 minutes and requires no new equipment.

How much does an AI receptionist cost for a small business?

Aria starts at $49/month for the Voice plan (200 minutes, 2 languages). The Site plan is $79/month (includes a custom website, web chat, and online booking). The Full plan is $149/month (unlimited minutes, 17 languages, custom knowledge base). All plans have a one-time setup fee that covers your initial configuration and training. No long-term contracts.

Can Aria handle Spanish-speaking callers?

Yes. Aria Voice supports English and Spanish out of the box. Aria Site adds French, Mandarin, and Portuguese for a total of 5 languages. Aria Full supports 17 languages including Korean, Arabic, Hindi, and more. Callers can switch languages mid-call naturally.

What's the difference between a virtual receptionist and an AI receptionist?

A traditional virtual receptionist is a remote human who answers calls during business hours — typically $200-400/month for limited coverage. An AI receptionist like Aria answers 24/7, never calls in sick, handles unlimited simultaneous calls, and costs a fraction of the price. Aria can also integrate with your website, booking system, and business knowledge base.

What happens if Aria can't help a caller?

Aria transfers the call to you or a team member, takes a detailed message, and sends you both a text and email notification. Nothing falls through the cracks — you always know who called and what they needed.

Do I need any special equipment for an AI answering service?

No. Aria works with your existing phone number — cell phone, VoIP, or landline. You don't need new hardware, software, or a landline. Setup takes about 10 minutes and your AI receptionist is live the same day.

Can I cancel my AI receptionist anytime?

Yes. There's no long-term contract. You can cancel or change plans at any time. The setup fee covers your initial configuration and training — it's a one-time cost, not a recurring charge.

How is Aria different from a regular answering service?

Traditional answering services just take messages. Aria is an AI-powered receptionist that understands your business, answers questions about your services and hours, books appointments in real-time, follows up with callers by text, and routes urgent calls to you. It's like having a trained receptionist who knows your business inside and out — for a fraction of the cost.
